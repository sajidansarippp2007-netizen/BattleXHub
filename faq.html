FAQ

❓ How do I join a tournament?

Select a tournament and tap Join. Coins will be deducted automatically.

💰 How do I withdraw coins?

Go to Wallet → Withdraw and submit your request.

🏆 When do I receive rewards?

Rewards are added after the admin publishes match results.

🚫 My room ID is not showing.

Room details appear a few minutes before the match starts.

📞 Need help?

Use the Contact Us option from the menu.
